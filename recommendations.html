To Whom It May Concern:

I'm writing to recommend Ryan Gray without reservation. I hired Ryan into an entry-level role at the Navy Exchange Service Command (NEXCOM) a little over a year ago, and it didn't take long to see what kind of employee I'd gotten. Three things stood out early: he pays attention to detail most people miss, he doesn't leave things half-done, and he shows up every day looking for ways to make things better — not just get through the day.

What's impressed me most is what Ryan has done beyond his actual job description. Over the past year he's improved several processes that had real, organization-wide impact, and he did it on his own initiative, on top of his regular workload, because he saw problems and wanted to fix them. A good example: our teams were struggling with a clunky manual pricing process, and Ryan automated it — no one asked him to.

He built something that tracks credits, generates fact tags, and handles a handful of other functions nobody had explicitly asked for — because he could see the tool needed to do more to be useful. And he built it fast.

That's really the thing about Ryan: how quickly he picks things up. Our internal systems aren't simple — I've watched experienced team members take years to get comfortable with tools Ryan mastered in a matter of months.

He came into an entry-level job, learned the fundamentals, and then just kept going. He's earned everything he has through work and curiosity, not luck or tenure. Ryan is a go-getter, and he's the kind of person who makes every team he is on better. I'd take him on any team of mine without a second thought, and I recommend him to you with full confidence.

Please feel free to contact me directly if you'd like to discuss Ryan's work further.

Sincerely,
